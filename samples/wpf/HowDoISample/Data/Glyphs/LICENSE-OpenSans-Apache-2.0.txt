Open Sans Semibold/0-255.pbf and 256-511.pbf are glyph ranges (signed distance fields) generated from
the Open Sans font, as served by https://demotiles.maplibre.org/font/ (the fonts of the OpenMapTiles
project, https://github.com/openmaptiles/fonts).
